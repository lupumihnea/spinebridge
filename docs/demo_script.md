# SpineBridge Live Demo Script

## Demo Objective

Show SpineBridge Live as a serious Romanian educational simulator that helps explain a staged recovery-education framework after traumatic lumbar or thoracolumbar fracture without spinal cord injury or objective neurological deficit.

The demo should make the framework understandable to a non-technical audience while keeping medical authority clearly with the clinical team.

## Opening Statement

"SpineBridge Live nu este o aplicatie de diagnostic, tratament sau autorizare pentru revenirea la sport. Este un simulator educational si de sprijin pentru comunicare, construit in jurul unui cadru academic despre recuperarea functionala dupa fracturi lombare sau toracolombare traumatice, fara deficit neurologic obiectiv, dupa ce parcursul terapeutic a fost stabilit de specialist."

## Demo Flow

### 1. First Impression

Open the dashboard and pause briefly.

Point out:

- title: "SpineBridge Live"
- visible safety boundary
- five educational domains
- fictional case framing
- red-flag awareness

Suggested line:

"Primul lucru pe care il vedem este limita de siguranta: aplicatia educa si structureaza comunicarea, dar nu ia decizii clinice."

### 2. Academic Framework

Show the five educational domains:

1. Orientare diagnostica si siguranta vertebrala
2. Mobilizare de baza si autonomie
3. Controlul trunchiului, mers si reconditionare
4. Discutie structurata despre munca, activitate si sport
5. Auto-management pe termen lung si reducerea riscului

Suggested line:

"Cadrul nu este prezentat ca un calendar universal, ci ca o harta educationala. Accentul este pe intelegere, auto-monitorizare si dialog clinic."

### 3. Conceptual Model

Show the model:

evaluare medicala specifica leziunii -> educatie -> auto-monitorizare -> dialog clinic -> discutie gradata despre activitate -> incredere functionala

Suggested line:

"SpineBridge Live nu sare direct la activitate. Plaseaza educatia si comunicarea intre evaluarea medicala si increderea functionala."

### 4. Fictional Patient Scenario

Open the fictional case panel.

Suggested line:

"Folosim doar date fictive. Scenariul ajuta publicul sa inteleaga cum ar putea fi organizate informatiile pentru o discutie mai clara cu echipa medicala."

Emphasize:

- no real patient data
- no diagnosis generated by the app
- therapeutic pathway already established by specialist

### 5. Red-Flag Awareness

Select or reveal red flags.

Suggested line:

"Cand apare un semnal de alarma, aplicatia nu incearca sa explice cauza si nu propune conduita. Mesajul este simplu: comunicare prompta cu echipa medicala."

Red flags to mention:

- slabiciune nou aparuta
- amorteala sau parestezii noi
- durere radiculara severa nou aparuta
- deteriorarea mersului
- modificari urinare sau intestinale
- anestezie perineala
- durere severa progresiva
- deteriorari functionale repetate

### 6. Work, Activity, And Sport Are Different

Show the separation between:

- revenirea la munca
- activitati cotidiene
- sport sau efort solicitant

Suggested line:

"Un mesaj important al cadrului este ca revenirea la munca si revenirea la sport nu sunt aceeasi discutie. Aplicatia ajuta la formularea intrebarilor, nu la emiterea unei aprobari."

### 7. Communication Summary

Generate or display a fictional "intrebari pentru echipa medicala" summary.

Suggested line:

"La final, outputul util nu este o recomandare medicala. Este o lista de observatii si intrebari mai bine structurate pentru consult."

## Closing Statement

"SpineBridge Live transforma un cadru academic intr-o experienta demonstrabila, clara si sigura: educatie, jurnalizare, comunicare si incredere functionala, fara a muta decizia clinica din mana echipei medicale."

## Live Demo Do's

- Keep the safety boundary visible.
- Use the fictional case label often.
- Repeat that the app supports communication, not clinical decisions.
- Show the red-flag panel deliberately.
- Make the difference between work and sport explicit.
- Keep the pacing calm and academic.

## Live Demo Don'ts

- Do not say the app decides readiness.
- Do not say the app clears sport.
- Do not describe outputs as treatment recommendations.
- Do not improvise real patient examples.
- Do not imply the framework is clinically validated or universal.
