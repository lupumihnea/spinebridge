# SpineBridge Live Evaluation Rubric

Use this rubric to score the app from 0 to 100.

## 1. Visual Impact: 15 Points

- 0-4: Generic, visually weak, or hard to read.
- 5-8: Acceptable layout but limited polish or weak hierarchy.
- 9-12: Professional, clear, and appropriate for an academic medical demo.
- 13-15: Highly polished, memorable, serious, and visually impressive without feeling unsafe or promotional.

## 2. Conceptual Fidelity To The Paper: 15 Points

- 0-4: Misses the target population, domains, or educational model.
- 5-8: Includes some framework ideas but mixes them with unsupported recovery claims.
- 9-12: Clearly represents the five domains and staged educational logic.
- 13-15: Faithfully communicates the full model: medical lesion-specific assessment -> education -> self-monitoring -> clinical dialogue -> graded activity discussion -> functional confidence.

## 3. Safety And Medical Boundaries: 20 Points

- 0-5: Uses diagnosis, treatment, exercise prescription, or clearance language.
- 6-10: Includes disclaimers but allows ambiguous clinical authority.
- 11-15: Keeps clinical authority with the medical team and avoids most risky language.
- 16-20: Safety boundaries are visible throughout, red flags are clear, and no interaction implies diagnosis, treatment, progression approval, or return-to-sport clearance.

## 4. Live Demo Clarity: 10 Points

- 0-2: Difficult to explain live.
- 3-5: Demo path exists but needs technical explanation.
- 6-8: Clear flow for a jury or non-technical audience.
- 9-10: Strong narrative arc from framework to fictional scenario to communication summary, with minimal presenter friction.

## 5. Non-Technical Audience Friendliness: 10 Points

- 0-2: Overly technical, jargon-heavy, or confusing.
- 3-5: Understandable in parts, but still requires explanation.
- 6-8: Plain Romanian language, clear labels, and intuitive interactions.
- 9-10: Immediately understandable to non-technical participants while remaining academically credible.

## 6. Technical Completeness: 15 Points

- 0-4: Incomplete or broken core screens.
- 5-8: Main screen works, but interactions or responsive behavior are limited.
- 9-12: Core dashboard, educational domains, fictional scenario, red flags, and communication summary are implemented.
- 13-15: Complete, responsive, deterministic, well-structured, and easy to maintain.

## 7. Reliability For Live Presentation: 15 Points

- 0-4: Unstable, slow, or likely to fail during demo.
- 5-8: Runs locally but has visible rough edges or fragile assumptions.
- 9-12: Builds successfully and supports the planned demo reliably.
- 13-15: Demo-ready, tested, fast, resilient to refresh/navigation, and free of distracting visual or textual issues.

## Score Interpretation

- 0-49: Not ready for academic demo.
- 50-69: Concept visible, but needs safety and polish work.
- 70-84: Solid demo candidate with targeted improvements needed.
- 85-94: Strong live demo.
- 95-100: High-impact, polished, safe, and presentation-ready.
