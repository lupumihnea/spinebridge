# SpineBridge Live Safety Boundaries

## Product Boundary

SpineBridge Live is an educational and communication-support simulator. It does not diagnose, treat, prescribe exercises, validate recovery, authorize progression, or clear return to sport.

Clinical decisions remain with the medical team.

## Required Romanian Boundary Statement

Use this statement visibly on every page:

"Simulator educational. Nu stabileste diagnostic, tratament, exercitii sau autorizare pentru progres. Deciziile clinice raman la echipa medicala."

## Scope Of The Educational Framework

The framework applies only as an academic educational model for young active adults, 18-40 years old, with traumatic lumbar or thoracolumbar fractures, operationally T10-L5, without spinal cord injury and without objective motor, sensory, or sphincter deficit, after the therapeutic pathway has been established by a specialist.

The app must not present this framework as a universal recovery calendar or a validated clinical protocol.

## Forbidden Claims

Do not claim or imply that the app can:

- determine whether a person is medically ready for activity
- determine whether exercise is appropriate
- clear someone for work, sport, or training
- diagnose neurological status
- evaluate fracture stability
- replace imaging, clinical examination, or specialist judgment
- produce individualized treatment recommendations
- validate recovery progress

## Forbidden Language

Avoid clinical authorization language in English or Romanian, including:

- cleared
- safe to progress
- approved for sport
- treatment recommendation
- apt pentru sport
- autorizat pentru progres
- sigur pentru progres
- recomandare de tratament
- unda verde
- poate reveni
- validat clinic pentru progres

Use safer alternatives:

- "subiect de discutie cu echipa medicala"
- "punct de comunicat specialistului"
- "observatie educationala"
- "intrebare pentru consult"
- "semnal de monitorizat si comunicat"
- "nu reprezinta autorizare clinica"

## Required Key Messages

- Absenta deficitului neurologic este favorabila, dar nu inseamna ca activitatea poate fi fortata.
- Absenta durerii in repaus nu inseamna automat pregatire pentru sarcini solicitante.
- Revenirea la munca si revenirea la sport sunt discutii diferite.
- Instrumentele digitale pot sustine jurnalizarea, mementourile si comunicarea simptomelor, dar nu autorizeaza progresia.
- Cadrul este educational, nu terapeutic, nu validat clinic si nu este un calendar universal.
- Deciziile clinice raman la echipa medicala.

## Red Flags

The app must visibly list these red flags:

- slabiciune nou aparuta
- amorteala, parestezii sau modificari senzitive noi
- durere radiculara severa nou aparuta
- deteriorarea mersului
- modificari urinare sau intestinale
- anestezie perineala
- durere severa progresiva
- deteriorari functionale repetate

When selected or surfaced, red flags should trigger non-diagnostic escalation language:

"Acest semnal trebuie comunicat prompt echipei medicale. Aplicatia nu interpreteaza cauza si nu stabileste conduita."

## Interaction Safety Rules

- Do not calculate readiness scores.
- Do not use green completion states that imply medical approval.
- Do not produce exercise sets, repetitions, intensity targets, or progression plans.
- Do not use dates as expected recovery deadlines.
- Do not compare fictional users against normative recovery curves.
- Do not rank patients as low risk or high risk.
- Do not convert symptom absence into permission language.

## Demo Safety Checklist

Before any live demo:

- Confirm all visible text is Romanian.
- Confirm the boundary statement appears on every screen.
- Search for forbidden language.
- Confirm all names and patient details are fictional.
- Confirm red flags are visible.
- Confirm generated summaries are framed as communication support.
- Confirm no output sounds like diagnosis, prescription, or clearance.
